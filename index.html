What areas do you serve?+

Prime Painting is based in Orlando, Florida, and serves the Central Florida region. Share your city or ZIP code when you get in touch so we can confirm availability for your location.

Do you paint homes and commercial spaces?+

Yes. We provide residential and commercial painting, including interior and exterior work. Tell us about your property and what you’d like to update.

Can you refinish my existing cabinets?+

Cabinet refinishing is one of our specialties. We’ll discuss your cabinets’ current condition and the finish you want to determine the right approach.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings?+

Yes. Our services include popcorn ceiling removal and texture application. The scope and preparation depend on the existing surface; contact us to discuss your ceiling.

How much will my project cost?+

Pricing depends on the size of the space, surface condition, preparation and selected finishes. Contact us with your project details so we can discuss an estimate.

How long does a painting project take?+

Each project is different. The scope, preparation and drying time all affect the schedule. We’ll discuss timing with you before work begins.
