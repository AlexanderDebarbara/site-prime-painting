Information on this website

The project message builder prepares a text message in your browser. If you choose Send request by email, the website submits your name, reply email, location, service and project description to our server and emails them to Prime Painting. The website does not save form submissions in a database. To limit abuse, the server may temporarily store a hashed identifier derived from your IP address and recent request times. SMS details leave the browser only if you choose to send a text through your messaging app.

When you contact us

If you call, text or email, Prime Painting receives the information you share, including the contact information provided by your telephone or messaging service. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Avoid including sensitive personal information in your project description.

Cookies and hosting

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. We use your browser’s local storage to remember that you acknowledged the cookie and privacy notice; you can clear this choice in your browser settings. The hosting provider may use essential cookies or similar technology and process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the website.

External services

Links to Instagram and your phone, messaging or email app take you outside this website. Those services handle information according to their own privacy practices.
